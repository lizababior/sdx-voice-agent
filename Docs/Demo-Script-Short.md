# VacMan: short demo script

About 75 seconds, split evenly between Jake, Liz and Deandre. Use the Johnson job. Each person speaks their own lines to VacMan.

## Intro (about 15 seconds)

- **Jake:** "This is VacMan, a voice assistant for HVAC techs. Maria has gloves on and her hands are full. She just talks, and the invoice is done before she leaves the driveway. I built the phone app and the live console."
- **Liz:** "I built the agent: the voice, the prompt, the shop knowledge and Spanish."
- **Deandre:** "I built the Daytona back end. It runs the refrigerant math and makes the invoice PDF."

## Demo (about 60 seconds)

Tap **Linda Johnson**, then tap the mic.

1. **Liz:** "Board's flashing three short, two long."
   Expect: code 32, low-pressure trip. Check airflow and charge.
2. **Deandre:** "Suction 105, suction line 60."
   Expect: "Running the numbers", then superheat about 26, so likely low charge. A calculation chip appears (run in Daytona).
3. **Deandre:** "Encontré la fuga en el Schrader, cambié el core, agregué dos libras."
   Expect: a Spanish answer. The notes appear in English.
4. **Jake:** "Also swapped the capacitor… actually no, it tested fine."
   Expect: the capacitor note disappears (change-of-mind curveball).
5. **Jake:** "Wrap up the job." Read-back, then **Jake:** "Yes."
   Expect: the invoice appears, VacMan reads the total, and the mic shuts off.

## Close

- **Liz:** "Notes, math and invoice, all hands-free, in English or Spanish."

Skip the off-topic curveball in the Loom to save time. At the live finals, the judges ask one anyway.
