# Coastal Comfort HVAC: shop policies

Coastal Comfort HVAC is a residential heating and air conditioning shop in San Diego, California.

## Hours

- Monday to Friday: 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
- Saturday: 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM.
- All other times are after hours.

## Pricing

- The shop uses flat-rate pricing from the price book. Each task or part has one fixed price, whatever the time on site.
- A technician cannot change a price in the field. For a discount or a price question, the technician calls the office.
- Every service call has a diagnostic fee (DIAG-STD).
- After-hours calls add an after-hours surcharge (DIAG-AH), on top of the diagnostic fee.
- Sales tax is 7.75% (San Diego) on the invoice subtotal.

## Warranty

- Shop labor on a repair: 1 year from the repair date.
- Parts: the manufacturer's warranty.
- Refrigerant added without a leak repair has no warranty.
- A repeat visit for the same problem within 30 days has no diagnostic fee.

## When to call the office

The technician calls the office before they continue when:

- The repair total is more than $1,500.
- The customer disputes a price or asks for a discount.
- The system needs more than 4 lb of refrigerant.
- The customer declines a leak search but asks for refrigerant.
- The technician finds a gas smell, a carbon monoxide alarm, or an electrical hazard. In this case, make the area safe first.

## Invoices

The technician's app creates the invoice at the end of the job and sends it to the office for review. The office sends it to the customer. Payment is due on receipt.
